# AX-17 Network and Account

## AX-17 network errors
On the AX-17, ERR-4021 means the network cable is disconnected. Reconnect the network cable, wait for the link light, and retry the connection.

## AX-17 login errors
On the AX-17, ERR-4022 means the account token expired. Sign out and then sign in again to get a fresh token.

## AX-17 password and reset
Use the account recovery link to reset a forgotten password. A factory reset deletes local settings. It is a separate procedure from password recovery.

## AX-17 firmware 2.1 recovery
Hold Power for 10 seconds, release it, then press Recovery. Wait until the status indicator flashes before releasing Recovery.

## AX-17 firmware 2.2 recovery
Hold Power for 5 seconds, then press Recovery. Wait until the status indicator turns blue.

## AX-17 blank display
If the display stays blank after boot, reconnect the display cable and restart the device once.

## AX-17 upload
The AX-17 accepts individual uploads up to 20 MB. Split larger attachments before uploading.

## AX-17 offline access
Local notes can be read offline, but cloud sync needs an internet connection. Notes already downloaded stay available while the device is offline.

## AX-17 diagnostics
Use send_logs() to export diagnostic logs. Save the export locally before attaching it to a support ticket.

## AX-17 battery and charging
Use the supplied 45 W USB-C charger. A steady amber light means the battery is still charging, while green means charging is complete.

## AX-17 storage
When the storage warning appears, move archived logs to an external drive. Leave at least 2 GB of free space before installing an update.
