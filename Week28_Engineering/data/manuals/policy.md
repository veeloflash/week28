# Service and Returns

## AX-17 standard returns
Standard AX-17 units can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Keep the order number when opening a return request.

## AX-17 custom unit exception
Custom-configured AX-17 units cannot be returned unless faulty. If a custom unit is faulty, contact support to start the service process.

## Warranty
The standard hardware warranty is 12 months from delivery. Accidental liquid damage is excluded.

## Shipping label
Support sends a prepaid return label after approving a return. Print the label and remove the original shipping label from the box.

## Support hours
The support team is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 local time. Weekend requests are handled on the next business day.

## Data before service
Back up your data and sign out before sending a device for repair. Keep your recovery code private.
