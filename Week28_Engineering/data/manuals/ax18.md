# AX-18 Service Manual

## AX-18 sensor errors
On the AX-18, ERR-4021 means the temperature sensor failed. Turn off the unit and contact support for service.

## AX-18 login errors
On the AX-18, ERR-4022 means the sign-in service is unavailable. Wait five minutes and retry.

## AX-18 upload
The AX-18 accepts individual uploads up to 40 MB. The limit also applies to attachments in support tickets.

## AX-18 display
If the AX-18 shows a flashing display after boot, switch to a certified display cable. The docking cable is a different part.

## AX-18 diagnostics
Use export_status() to save a device health snapshot. The export excludes user documents.

## AX-18 offline access
Cached status screens are visible offline. The device cannot download a new status snapshot without a connection.

## AX-18 charging
Use the supplied 65 W charger. Disconnect the charger if the plug is damaged.
